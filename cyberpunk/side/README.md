# cyberpunk — side index

Non-canon pieces: one-shots, what-ifs, whump and fluff that don't have to fit the timeline. Nothing in `info/` or `arc/` depends on anything here.

Idea backlog: [ideas.md](ideas.md). A piece without an `outline.md` uses its idea bullet as the premise.

Tags: `slice-of-life`, `whump` — they mark tone, not genre. A piece can have neither (a straight job story, say); note an AU in "Assumes". "Premise" is the bullet title in `ideas.md`, or `outline.md` if the piece has one. "Assumes" notes roughly where a piece would sit and anything it takes for granted.
