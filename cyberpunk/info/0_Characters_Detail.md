# 0_Characters_Detail

Lookup-only detail behind the character bible: the mechanics, history, and specific tells of entries that the main character file states in short form. Headings follow the main file.

# Main Cast

## Evelyn

### Mental State

- Her line on losing body parts: "Worst case, I lose another piece. Then I'll get a new one."
- When no one is watching, she still presses on a bruise or deliberately walks on a sprained ankle.

### Backstory

- At 17, not long after losing her right eye, she sold a full 3D scan of her face to Concord's template bank. The replacement eye had gone onto her implant debt, and by then a face was just another part. She took cash instead of the debt credit, and signed the release without reading it. She wouldn't have cared what it said.

### Hobbies

- Her actual singing has been banned by the landlord. It's decent, just too loud — she sings everything like a military anthem.

## Victoria

### Backstory

- Skull reinforcement was the only reason the blast wasn't fatal.
- With no 3D scan of her original face to rebuild from, the reconstruction used Concord's template bank, the standard and cheapest option. The match ran on what her file recorded — blond hair, blue eyes, skin tone.

### Hobbies

- Evelyn knows she's reading when she stares into the void for too long.
- She may or may not have been writing her own novel and active on certain online forums, but she won't admit it.

### Personalized Implants

- The drone feed shows in the same corner of both eyes, so it reads as one image. When their calibration drifts, the two windows stop lining up, which is what causes the eye strain and headaches on long jobs.
- The drone link is display-only: she locked it down so a hijacked drone can't send anything else into her eyes.
- Drone commands run on a separate channel from her voice to Evelyn.

# Relationship

## Resemblance

- The likeness is most obvious when Victoria's lower face is covered.
- Evelyn cuts her own hair and insists on trimming Victoria's bangs too — otherwise Victoria would never bother to style them — which sometimes results in them having similar bangs.
- When people ask if they're related, Evelyn's usual answer is "close enough."
- People who ask if they're related usually just assume they are, don't really care, or are respectful enough not to ask further.

## Mutual Awareness

- Victoria can spot even a minor injury on Evelyn from her gait or how she holds herself.

## Vitals & Location Link

- They repurposed their vitals monitors and location pings after leaving the unit.
- The location ping runs passively at all times.
- Vitals are threshold-only outside of jobs: the other person gets an alert only if readings cross a set threshold. Neither can browse the other's data freely.
- Live vitals viewing is switched on manually by both of them at the start of a job and switched off once they're clear.
- The visual link into Evelyn's artificial eye only opens when both of them agree, which in practice means during jobs, alongside live vitals.

# Side Cast

## Carol

- Got her direct brain interface through one of Axion's programs, a network-focused one.
- Walked out of the program on bad terms while it was still running. She's talented enough to have gotten away with it.
- Still runs on Axion's firmware, but she has dealt with the backdoor herself.

## Blake

- Needed a direct brain interface for medical reasons. Axion's experimental program for remote-piloted frames was the only option he had. The program was later canceled, and he left Axion when it was.
- Still runs on Axion's firmware.
- Civilian employers are put off by the frame, and ordinary wages wouldn't cover upkeep anyway.
- The frame is built for fighting, so that's where he's most useful.
